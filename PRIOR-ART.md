

## Search of 15 November 2026 (independent agent, `verification/agent-2026-11-15/REPORT.md`)

Luo's revised fence bound is in A059442 (comment of 8 Jan 2024) and was posted on Mathematics Stack Exchange
(question 4812195, 22 Nov 2023, revised 2 Dec 2023). Misha Lavrov's answer there (27 Nov 2023) refutes the original
equality through R(3,n) = O(n^2/log n), not the revised upper bound. Erdos (Bull. AMS 53, 1947) gives the classical
lower bound used here. No earlier refutation of the revised bound was found (OEIS, MSE, MathOverflow, arXiv,
web-indexed zbMATH); the OEIS history page returned HTTP 403, so the search is not exhaustive.
